# Complete initial Gluons automation skill catalog

Every skill has SKILL.md, machine-readable workflow.yaml, and evals.json. These are independent specifications requiring implementation and real testing before production.

1. **multichannel_order_entry** — Food distribution — Customer message, voice transcript, or scanned order sheet → ERP order draft, customer confirmation after approval (reference market example: Asakana; risk: medium)
2. **restaurant_inventory_planning** — Restaurants — Daily forecast schedule or new stock/sales/invoice data → Inventory snapshot, suggested PO, prep plan and staffing forecast (reference market example: Marble; risk: high)
3. **restaurant_voice_ordering** — Restaurants — Inbound restaurant phone call → Verified POS order or reservation and call outcome (reference market example: Certus AI; risk: high)
4. **property_maintenance** — Property management — Tenant phone, message, email or maintenance portal request → Resolved ticket, contractor evidence and approved invoice (reference market example: Brickwise; risk: high)
5. **freight_order_to_cash** — Transportation — Freight order email, EDI, PDF or portal event → TMS job, customer updates, POD and draft invoice (reference market example: Lunavo; risk: high)
6. **title_closing_support** — Real estate title — New purchase contract or settlement email → Draft title file, discrepancies and review-ready closing documents (reference market example: Vestris; risk: regulated)
7. **workflow_discovery** — Horizontal operations — Consented local event observation, uploaded process log or workflow interview → Suggested automations with evidence, consent and sandbox evaluation (reference market example: Cofia; risk: high)
8. **ecommerce_returns** — E-commerce — Return request, delivery exception or support ticket → Verified RMA, exchange, refund and inventory update (reference market example: Pango; risk: high)
9. **shopping_guidance** — E-commerce — Shopper inquiry or catalog/product-page interaction → Grounded recommendation and storefront guidance; checkout stays with store (reference market example: Kinect; risk: medium)
10. **local_creator_campaigns** — Local marketing — Business approves creator campaign plan → Booked creator visits, published campaign links and transparent report (reference market example: TryNearby; risk: medium)
11. **field_service_opportunity_detection** — Home services — Technician visit closes or service history updates → Qualified follow-on opportunity and owner-approved follow-up (reference market example: Robby; risk: medium)

13. **construction_estimates** — Construction — Bid invitation and drawings/specification upload → Traceable quantity takeoff, estimate and bid draft (reference market example: FlowManual; risk: high)

19. **commercial_insurance_submission** — Insurance — Broker receives client risk information or renewal packet → Carrier-specific draft submission and discrepancy list (reference market example: Casey; risk: regulated)
