# Inventory reorder review

Ask the owner to confirm the stock location, the currency and the rules for each item. Capture the supplier, threshold, target stock, pack size, per-item draft limit, and estimated price where known. Save these rules to the draft automation so later snapshots use the same assumptions.

Use a synthetic or owner-supplied snapshot with available and incoming quantities for that location. Review missing or duplicate counts, missing estimates, and quantities that exceed the draft limit. Report results as draft purchases in the configured currency, with the snapshot time and evidence basis. Purchase orders, payments, and supplier messages require their own authorized workflows and provider evidence.
